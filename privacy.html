Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

WristNav is a Wear OS application for offline map navigation and GPS activity tracking. This privacy policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, how it is shared, and your rights.

Data We Collect

WristNav may collect and process the following data on your device:

  • Location data — GPS coordinates used for map display and route recording
  • Health data — Heart rate and step count collected from your watch's sensors during activity recording
  • Activity data — Distance, pace, elevation, heart rate, steps and duration of recorded activities
  • GPX files — Route files you import or that are generated from your recordings

Health Data

WristNav may access health-related data from your Wear OS watch when you use activity recording features. This includes heart rate measured by the watch's heart-rate sensor and step count provided by the watch's activity sensors or supported health-data services.

How health data is collected: Health data is collected when you use WristNav's activity-recording features and grant the required permissions. Heart rate is obtained from the watch's heart-rate sensor and step information is obtained from supported activity data sources.

How health data is used: Heart rate and step-count data are used to provide live activity statistics and to record activity information. Where available, this information is included in the GPX file generated by WristNav.

How health data is stored: WristNav stores the recorded activity information locally on your device as part of the activity and GPX data. WristNav does not operate its own server for storing your health data.

How health data is shared: Health data may leave the device only when you explicitly use a feature that transfers your activity data to another service. WristNav supports transferring recorded activity data to Health Connect and Intervals.icu.

  • Health Connect: When you choose to use Health Connect integration, WristNav sends supported activity and health information from the recorded GPX/activity data to Health Connect. Health Connect is operated by Google and its own privacy controls and policies apply.
  • Intervals.icu: When you choose to upload an activity to Intervals.icu, WristNav uploads the GPX file containing the recorded activity data. This may include GPS route information, heart rate, steps, elevation, distance, pace, duration and other information contained in the GPX file.
  • Strava: WristNav does not directly upload activities to Strava. If you want to use an activity with Strava, you must first download the GPX file and then manually upload that file to Strava yourself. WristNav does not send health data directly to Strava.

WristNav does not sell health data and does not use health data for advertising, advertising profiling, or unrelated purposes.

How We Use Your Data

WristNav processes your data to provide offline navigation and activity-recording features.

  • Location data is used to display your position on the map and record your route
  • Health data is used to display live activity statistics and record relevant activity information
  • Activity data is used to provide distance, pace, elevation, heart-rate, step-count and duration statistics
  • GPX files are generated from recorded activities and stored on your device
  • When you explicitly use an integration, activity data may be transferred to Health Connect or Intervals.icu as described in this policy

Health Connect

WristNav provides optional integration with Health Connect. If you grant the required permissions and choose to use this integration, WristNav can send supported activity and health information from your recorded activities to Health Connect.

The information transferred may include data such as exercise/activity information, heart rate, steps, distance, elevation, duration and other supported information contained in the recorded activity.

Health Connect is a platform provided by Google. Data stored in Health Connect is subject to Google's Health Connect functionality, permissions and privacy practices. WristNav does not receive or sell your Health Connect data for advertising purposes.

Intervals.icu Integration

WristNav offers optional integration with Intervals.icu. If you choose to connect your Intervals.icu account and upload an activity, WristNav uploads the recorded GPX file to Intervals.icu.

The uploaded GPX file may contain all data recorded by WristNav for that activity, including GPS coordinates, route information, heart rate, steps, elevation, distance, pace and duration.

The transfer occurs only when you choose to upload the activity. Data received by Intervals.icu is subject to Intervals.icu's own privacy policy and terms.

Strava

WristNav does not provide direct activity uploading to Strava.

If you want to use a WristNav activity with Strava, you can download the GPX file generated by WristNav and manually upload that file to Strava using Strava's own website or application. WristNav does not send the activity directly to Strava and does not store your Strava credentials or access tokens.

Once you manually upload a GPX file to Strava, the data contained in that file is subject to Strava's own privacy policy. See strava.com/legal/privacy.

Data Sharing

WristNav does not sell your personal data. Data may be transferred to third-party services only when you explicitly use the relevant feature:

  • Health and activity data may be transferred to Health Connect when you choose to use the Health Connect integration
  • A GPX file containing activity data may be uploaded to Intervals.icu when you choose to upload an activity
  • A GPX file may be manually shared using your device's share functionality
  • A downloaded GPX file may be manually uploaded by you to Strava
  • A crash log may be manually sent to the developer via email when you choose to do so

WristNav does not share health data with advertising networks or use health data for advertising purposes.

Data Storage

WristNav stores recorded activity and GPX data locally on your device. GPX recordings are saved to your device's Downloads folder. Data transferred to Health Connect or Intervals.icu is stored according to the respective service's policies and settings.

You can delete locally stored data by removing the relevant files from your device or uninstalling the app. Data that has already been transferred to a third-party service must be managed or deleted through that service.

Permissions

WristNav requests the following permissions where required by the features you use:

  • Location — for GPS tracking and map display
  • Body Sensors — for heart rate monitoring during activities
  • Activity Recognition — for step counting during activities
  • Health Connect — where you choose to transfer supported activity and health data to Health Connect
  • Storage / file access — to save and read GPX files where supported by the Android version and storage model

Analytics

WristNav uses Firebase Analytics to collect usage data. This helps us understand how the app is used and improve the experience. The following data may be collected:

  • Screens and features visited within the app
  • Device model, manufacturer and Android version
  • App version and build number
  • Country and language settings
  • Session duration and frequency of use

No GPS coordinates, health data, activity data or GPX files are sent to Firebase Analytics. Analytics is not used to collect or analyse health information. Firebase's own privacy policy applies. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Crash Reporting

WristNav uses Sentry to automatically collect crash reports. When the app crashes or encounters an error, the following technical data may be sent to Sentry's servers:

  • Stack trace and error type
  • Device model, manufacturer and Android version
  • App version and build number
  • Approximate geographic location (city/country level, derived from IP address)
  • Device hardware information (memory, storage, screen size)
  • A randomly generated anonymous user ID (not linked to any personal identity)
  • Battery level and charging state at time of crash

No GPS coordinates, heart rate, step count, other health data, activity data or GPX files are intentionally sent to Sentry. Crash reports are used solely to identify and fix bugs. Sentry's own privacy policy applies to data processed by Sentry. See sentry.io/privacy.

Some crashes are not captured automatically by Sentry. As a backup, WristNav includes a button that lets you manually send a crash log to the developer via email. This only happens if you tap the button and choose to send it; it is never sent automatically. The crash log contains technical information about what happened at the time of the crash. WristNav does not intentionally include GPS coordinates, health data, activity data, GPX files, or other personal information in this crash log.
