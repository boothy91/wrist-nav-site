WristNav

A waypoint-by-waypoint map of every screen, gesture, and setting in the watch app.

Opens on
The Map screen, directly. First launch prompts a map download — a local map is suggested from your current GPS position.
Hold
On the Map screen, press and hold to open the Navigation menu. On any other screen, press and hold to open the Global menu.
Menu icon
A dedicated button on the Map screen also opens the Global menu directly.
Note
Settings content below is carried over unchanged from the previous flow — flag anything that needs updating.
