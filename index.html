⚠️ Update & Known Issues
Upcoming Update: An update was planned to be sent for review today. However, a bug was identified during final testing. A fix has now been implemented, but it needs further testing before the update can be submitted for review.
Map Style: There is currently an issue when changing map styles where the app theme can turn blue and switch to the Fjord theme. This does not mean the map itself has turned blue.
Map Style Workaround:
  1. Change the map style to Topo.
  2. The app theme may turn blue.
  3. Close WristNav completely.
  4. Re-open WristNav.
  5. Topo should now be selected correctly.
Heart Rate: Heart-rate recording is currently affected by a permission issue in the current release. The issue has been identified and is fixed in the next update.

Features

🗺️

Offline Maps

Download maps directly to your Wear OS watch and navigate without relying on your phone or a mobile data connection.

⛰️

TOPO Maps

Access downloadable topographic maps from OpenTopoMap, SwissTopo, Tracestrack, USGS and Kartverket, with a resizable download area and direct watch synchronisation.

🌄

Hillshading

Add a hillshading overlay to Mapsforge maps to make terrain, slopes and the shape of the landscape easier to understand.

📍

GPS Route Recording

Record your activities directly on the watch with GPS, distance, speed, elevation and other activity data.

📁

GPX Import & Navigation

Import GPX routes and follow them directly from your watch, with your route displayed over the map.

💾

GPX Export

Export recorded routes as GPX files so you can keep, share or use your routes elsewhere.

📈

Elevation & Climb Tracking

Monitor elevation, ascent and descent during your activity, making WristNav useful for hiking and other outdoor activities.

❤️

Heart Rate Tracking

Record heart-rate data alongside your activity. A permission issue affecting heart-rate recording in the current release has been fixed for the next update.

👣

Step Tracking

Track steps during recorded activities alongside your route and other activity statistics.

📊

Customisable Statistics

Configure your statistics pages to display the information that matters most to you while you're moving.

⌚

Watch Face Complications

Display live WristNav information directly on compatible Wear OS watch faces using complications.

🔄

Health Connect Integration

Sync activity information with Health Connect without requiring a WristNav cloud account.

🖼️

Route Snapshots

Create shareable route images from your recorded activities, including the route displayed over your own photos.

🔗

Map Downloads via URL

Download maps using a direct URL and transfer them to your watch without having to rely on a fixed selection of sources.

🎨

Custom Map Themes

Import custom map theme XML files and transfer them directly to the watch.

🧭

Standalone Navigation

WristNav is designed to provide navigation directly from your Wear OS watch, without requiring your phone during your activity.

🚫

No Subscription

No monthly subscription and no mandatory WristNav account. Use the app without being locked into a recurring payment.

Downloadable TOPO Maps Beta

WristNav now supports downloadable topographic maps from OpenTopoMap, SwissTopo, Tracestrack, USGS and Kartverket. Choose a map source, resize the download area to suit your route, download it and sync it directly to your watch. Go to Map Settings → Map Style to switch between available map styles.

Hillshading for Mapsforge Beta

A new hillshading overlay is available for Mapsforge maps, providing additional terrain detail and making slopes and elevation changes easier to identify at a glance.

Tracestrack Map Source

Tracestrack maps require your own free API key. You can obtain one from the Tracestrack console.
