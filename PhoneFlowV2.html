WristNav Companion

Everything the phone app does — sending routes, planning, and watch extras. The watch works fully standalone; this app is optional.

Tabs
Send · Routes · Plan · Watch · Settings — five tabs across the bottom of the app.
Optional
Offline maps, GPX routes and GPS recording all work directly on the watch. The phone app adds planning, sync, and extras.
